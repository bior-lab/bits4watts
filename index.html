Welcome to Bits4Watts

International Workshop on Low-Carbon Building and Urban Energy Systems Powered by Data, AI, and Smart Controls

Bits4Watts brings together researchers and practitioners to explore how data, artificial intelligence, and smart controls can make buildings and urban energy systems more energy-efficient and low-carbon.

Our workshops feature case studies, recent research, and open discussion on building energy management, from individual buildings to communities. We connect advances in data management, diagnostics, control technologies, and grid interactions with their practical application.
